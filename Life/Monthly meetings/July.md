# July

## What am I grateful for this month?

I’m grateful that I got my job at Thursday Strategy, and grateful that the team seems like an amazing group of people. I’m grateful for my time in New York City, and I’m grateful that my parents were willing to let me come home early when I needed to. I’m grateful for my friends and family, especially Madi, Reagan, Stella, and Taylor. I’m grateful for the fun I’ve had since getting back to North Carolina. I’m grateful that I only have one semester (less than five months!) left at UNC.

## What were my wins this month?

Getting out of my relationship, spending time with family and friends, and landing my job at Thursday.

## What challenges were in my control?

My relationship falling apart (on my end), which I could’ve handled better. Giving notice at my internship, which I feel like I could’ve handled better, but it was also an odd situation.

## What challenges were out of my control?

My relationship falling apart (on his end). The office environment at my internship. John’s manipulation tactics as I tried to search for other jobs.

## What do I need to stop doing?

Searching for approval from others that I should be giving to myself. Letting others’ opinions of me, positive or negative, determine my self worth. Obsessing over the past as a problem to fix. Torturing myself with my past mistakes. Doing for others what I want them to do for me, like buying presents or apologizing. 

## What do I need to start doing?

Praying more regularly and going to church. Exercising. Developing a better, healthier diet. Figure out how to budget my money. Learning to say no and walk away from situations that aren’t right for me. Learning to trust that giving something good up doesn’t mean I’ll never have anything good again - just because something is “good enough” does not mean I should stick around until it gets bad. 

## What should I continue doing?

Expressing my emotions proactively. Creative things: writing on paper, drawing, taking pictures, etc. Praying. Having fun with family and friends. Working hard. Choosing to stay positive. 

## What are my main goals for next month? 

Making progress with my health (diet and exercise in particular). Praying daily. Settling in to my routine at Thursday. Growing a better backbone. Learning to be confident in myself regardless of my circumstances. Learning to give up things that are just good enough. Consistently working on my book.
