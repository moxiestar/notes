### **Big thoughts I have**

- I always want to predict things so I can feel more in control, but this is rarely helpful, because I feel so upset when my predictions don’t come to pass.
- I struggle with not knowing how things are going to turn out. I handle uncertainty fine if I’m not attached to any particular outcome, but once I start getting attached to an outcome, the uncertainty starts driving me crazy, and I get this desire to have everything figured out. I want things to go the way I want, and I want everything to be done. I don’t want to have to stay in the uncertain situation.
- I get so obsessed with certain things - people, possessions, opportunities - that I end up not being able to think straight or see things rationally. And often I don’t actually want those things, I just want the comfort of knowing I have them, or knowing that others know that I have them.
- I’m only disciplined when it comes to things that other people enforce, like my grades or my work. If the discipline only benefits me, I won’t follow through, I’ll always give in to my urges or my bad habits. And when I’m bored and lonely and hurt, like I am now, I will always want to give in to my urges and bad habits.
- I have a very event-based mindset. For example, “once I graduate, my life will change, and I’ll be able to build new habits.” But in reality, graduating is arbitrary, and I can change at any time, I just don’t want to.
- I’m afraid that if I change, I’ll be leaving a lot of my life behind, including my family, and my family needs me.
- I act crazy because I have been punished and excluded for being too good, too smart, too polished. 

### **Big questions I have**

1. How can I change and grow and work towards good things if I’ve gotten so used to disappointing things, things that aren’t right for me?
2. How can I expect consistent sympathy and understanding and respect if I’ve never really had it from the most important people in my life?
3. How can I move on from the things and people I have now if I have nothing to move forward to? How do I move forward if the only thing I’m moving towards is myself?
4. How can I do anything but what I’ve done my whole life: wait for people to change while tolerating hurtful behavior over and over again?
5. If I accept a disappointing relationship from my parents, how can I even try to build a good relationship with anyone else?
6. How do I do any of this? How do I take away my bad habits if I don’t have good habits yet? And how do I build those good habits?
7. How do I benefit from continuing these habits and patterns?
