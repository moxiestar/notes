#### **Lecture | Course structure**

**Three main focuses**

- Spatial (location, environment, etc)
- Data (processing, organizing, etc)
- Science (research, etc)

**Spatial thinking**

- Emphasizes an interconnected view of data
- Spatial autocorrelation: idea that things are more related the closer they are
- Scale: level of detail in the data
- Spatial heterogeneity: idea that relationships between variables change across different areas
