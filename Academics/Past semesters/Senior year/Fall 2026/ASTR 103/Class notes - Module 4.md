# ASTR 103 | Module 4

## **Lecture Twenty-Eight | Silurian Hypothesis**

**Lifetime of our civilization**

- All buildings should erode in less than a million years
- **Fossils**
  - Each dinosaur species left approximately 0.2 fossils per million years
- **Most of what we leave behind will be litter (pills, phone parts, fertilizer, plastic)**

**Silurian Hypothesis**

- **Is it possible that another technological civilization once inhabited Earth but left no discoverable remains?**
  - Yes!

**Suess Effect**

- **Isotopes**
  - Atoms that belong to the same element, but have different numbers of neutrons in their nuclei
  - A superscript number indicates the total number of protons and neutrons in an atom of a given isotope
  - **Carbon isotopes**
    - Most of the carbon on earth is carbon-12
    - Only 1% is carbon-13
    - Either isotope can form CO2
    - **Living organisms preferentially incorporate carbon-12 over carbon-13, so the carbon in fossil fuels is 99% carbon-12**
      - Burning fossil fuels adds CO2 to the atmosphere, but the fraction of CO2 made with carbon-13 goes down
