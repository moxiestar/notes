**Suess Effect**

- **Isotopes**
  - Atoms that belong to the same element, but have different numbers of neutrons in their nuclei
  - A superscript number indicates the total number of protons and neutrons in an atom of a given isotope
  - **Carbon isotopes**
    - Most of the carbon on earth is carbon-12
    - Only 1% is carbon-13
    - Either isotope can form CO2
    - **Living organisms preferentially incorporate carbon-12 over carbon-13, so the carbon in fossil fuels is 99% carbon-12**
      - Burning fossil fuels adds CO2 to the atmosphere, but the fraction of CO2 made with carbon-13 goes down
    - **Climate change correlated with carbon**
      - **Paleocene Eocene Thermal Maximum**
        - Spike in temperature correlated to lower rates of carbon-13
      - **Eocene Layer of Mysterious Origin**
        - Spike in temperature correlated to lower rates of carbon-13
      - **Neither of these are likely caused by another civilization, though**
- **Any advanced civilization probably found ways to reduce its environmental impact, making it harder to detect in the future**

## Lecture Thirty-One | ‘Oumuamua

**Pan-STARRs Telescope**

- **Telescope searches for objects in the sky**
  - In 2017, they found an unusual object
  - While stars left trails in the sky, this object didn’t
  - This abnormality occurred because it wasn’t orbiting the sun
- **Eccentricity**
  - **How much an ellipse deviates from a perfect circle**
    - e = 0 for a perfect circle
    - e = 1 for a squashed ellipse
- **‘Oumuamua**
  - Hawaiian name for “messenger from afar arriving first”
  - **The first interstellar object ever discovered by humanity**
    - It was about 100m in size and reddish-brown in color
    - Consistent with organic-rich surfaces and iron-rich minerals
  - **Its trajectory brought it into the solar system (within 0.25AU of the sun)**
    - ‘Oumuamua was travelling towards the same point that the solar system is travelling towards
    - It was travelling at the same speed as the solar system (26km/s)
    - It passed through the solar system in a month
    - **We don’t know where it came from**
  - **‘Oumuamua’s light curve indicated thats its brightness changed by a factor of 10 every few hours**
    - **Light curves of rotating objects**
      - An oblong object will change size and shape as it rotates
      - If the longer side was facing Earth, the object’s surface area would seem much larger
    - **‘Oumuamua is likely oblong, with one side being 10x longer than the other**
      - But this is highly atypical… Scully, you’re not gonna believe this
  - **There is also some evidence that ‘Oumuamua might have been propelled through the sky**
    - The difference been the expected trajectory and the observed trajectory was small but legitimate
      - Its trajectory indicates that it may have been propelled by a light sail, a contraption that uses light for propulsion
    - **It could have been acting like a comet, with ices subliminating from its surface**
      - There was no observable tail, but it might have been difficult to detect
    - Statistically, there should be $10^{15}$ objects like ‘Oumuamua per solar system
  - **Because of all this, some astronomers argue that ‘Oumuamua was an alien artifact or UFO**
    - Its shape is strange
    - It passed suspiciously close to Earth
    - We saw signs of non-gravitational acceleration
    - It had a suspiciously mysterious point of entry to solar system
  - **But it’s probably not an alien artifact**
    - Its shape was odd, but it could’ve formed naturally
    - Its proximity to Earth had no significance - anything could come close to Earth
    - Its acceleration is odd, but can be explained (see above)
    - Its behavior was pretty consistent with rock behavior
  - **Since 2017, two interstellar comets, similar to ‘Oumuamua, have passed through the solar system**

## Lecture Thirty-Two | Pareidolia & Ventifacts

**Life on Mars?**

- **Natural oddities**
  - Cracks that appear on Mars’ surface are just cracks in the dry mud
- **Mars Curiosity Rover**
  - SUV-sized rover that scientists have used to map most of Mars’ surface
  - Photographs have captured some unusual phenomena, but most can be explained
    - A strange flare was actually a cosmic ray hit (charged particles hitting detectors)
    - A possible Martian base was actually patterns caused by wind in the sand
    - Spirals on Mars petroglyphs were actually patterns left by hardened lava
- **Surface of Mars = rocks on sand**

**Pareidolia**

- Tendency for us to perceive patterns in neutral/meaningless stimuli
- Often, we identify human faces in non-human objects

**Ventifacts**

- Rocks carved by wind, typically found in areas where little erosion occurs

**Lunar rilles**

- Features on the Moon caused by lava tubes
- Straight rilles come from faults

**UFOs**

- **Unidentified Flying Object**, a term usually used to refer to possible alien spacecraft
  - UAP (Unidentified Aerial Phenomena) is an alternate term for UFOs
- **UFOs are often identifiable as planets, stars, airplanes, balloons, clouds, etc**
  - **Bokeh:** phenomenon where out-of-focus objects take on the shape of the camera aperture
  - **Diffraction rings:** phenomenon where out-of-focus objects seem to have concentric circles)
  - **Ball lightning:** phenomenon where balls of light appear and travel through space
    - Extremely rare (5% of the population has observed it) but legitimate scienfitic phenomenon
  - **Image compression:** compression of image files to shrink the file size, which sometimes causes pixelated “auras”
    - **Lossless compression:** repeating pixels to make files smaller
    - **Lossy compression:** removing pixels to make files smaller
- **Autokinetic effect:** phenomenon where stationary objects appear to move
  - Caused by random eye movements and a lack of reference objcets
- **Camera resolution**
  - We want to calculate the smallest details we can observe when capturing a distant object
  - **Resolution of the object** = **the resolution of the camera** times **the distance to the object**
