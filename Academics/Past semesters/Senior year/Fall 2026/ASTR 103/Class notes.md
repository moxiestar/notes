#### **Lecture | Life**

**What is life?**

- Organisms that grow, reproduce, respond to stimuli, regulate their internal environment, and use external energy

**What are atoms?**

- Everything we can see is made of atoms
- **Structure of atoms**
  - **Outer shell:** electrons
  - **Inner shell:** protons and neutrons
- Atoms can have positive, negative, or neutral charge
- Electrons can only orbit their atoms in certain shells

**What are molecules?**

- When multiple atoms are bound together by attractive forces, they form a molecule
- **Covalent bond:** chemical bond where two atoms share a pair of electrons
  - ==🔴Number of possible covalent bonds is determined by the number of empty spots in the atom’s valence shell==

**Why is carbon important for life?**

- A single carbon atom can form 4 covalent bonds, making it the backbone of many essential complex molecules
- Carbon helps build many essential amino acids and proteins, including DNA & RNA
- Silicon has a similar structure (ability to form 4 covalent bonds), so some scientists speculate that silicon-based life could be possible

**What is a solvent?**

- **Solvent:** liquid in which another substance dissolves
  - **Example:** salt dissolving in water
- Water is an excellent solvent because of its polarity (one side of the molecule is positive and the other side is negative)
  - Ammonia has been proposed as an alternate solvent

**What is abiogenesis?**

- **Abiogenesis:** formation of life from non-living elements
  - We don’t really understand how this happens
  - **Panspermia:** theory that life formed elsewhere in the universe and then travelled to earth
- **Steps**
  1. Production of organic molecules
  2. Replication of organic molecules (i.e., RNA replicates DNA)

#### **Lecture | Drake Equation**

![{\\displaystyle N=R_{*}\\cdot f_{\\mathrm {p} }\\cdot n_{\\mathrm {e} }\\cdot f_{\\mathrm {l} }\\cdot f_{\\mathrm {i} }\\cdot f_{\\mathrm {c} }\\cdot L}](https://wikimedia.org/api/rest_v1/media/math/render/svg/08459525b4c05af9b9e1748406e26ad869d9462d)

$N$: number of civilizations in the Milky Way that can communicate across interstellar distances

$R_*$: rate at which stars form in the Milky Way

$f_p$: fraction of stars with planets

$n_e$: for stars with planets, the average number of potentially habitable planets

$f_l$: fraction of habitable planets with life

$f_i$: fraction of habitable planets with life that develop intelligent life

$f_c$: fraction of intelligent civilizations that develop the ability to communicate across interstellar distances

$L$: average lifetime of a communicating civilization
