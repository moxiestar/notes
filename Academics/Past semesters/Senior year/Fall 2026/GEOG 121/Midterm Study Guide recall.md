# Midterm Study Guide | Recall

**Globalization**

- Process of people, things, and ideas becoming more interconnected

**Globality**

- Flows of people, things, and ideas across geographically distinct areas of the Earth

**Global imaginary**

- Mental understanding of globalization

**Globalism**

- Political portrayals of the Earth as globalized and interconnected

**Blue Marble**

- Most circulated image of all time: photograph of the Earth from space, captured by astronauts on an Apollo mission

**Transplanetary**

- Connections between people or things across different areas of the Earth

**Supraterritorial**

- Connections between people or things that transcend distinct territorial boundaries

**Time-space compression**

- Sense of distance and time shrinking due to global interconnectedness

**==🔴Global sense of place (Massey)==**

- Understanding individual places as related to an overarching sense of globality

**Differentiated mobility and power geometry (Massey)**

- The theory that different people experience globality differently; because of power differences, some have greater freedom and mobility than others

**Cognitive vs. mental maps**

- A mental map is an internal understanding of a place, while a cognitive map relates a place to a specific task or routine

**Global scapes (Appadurai)**

- Appadurai names five global scapes: ethnoscapes, the flow of people across the earth; ideoscapes, the flow of media; technoscapes, the flow of information and technology; mediascapes, the flow of media; and financescapes
