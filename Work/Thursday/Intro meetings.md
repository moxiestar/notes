# Introductory meeting with Brandon | August 7, 2026

### What’s your role at Thursday?

Associate on the quantitative team, supporting leads or directors on particular projects.

### What Thursday project are you most proud of? Walk me through the methodology and final report.

**Chase Freedom QCEP Calendar Optimization project**

- Researched the two Freedom cards (rotating quarterly benefits categories) to figure out what benefits configurations participants liked best
- Used MaxDiff (ranking competitor cards), TURF analysis (finding smallest possible bundles to reach as many customers as possible), BYO (exercise where participants built customized benefits calendar)
- Results were as expected - people favored Amazon, groceries, dining, etc
- Recommended prioritizing simplicity (fewer details) and grocery/shopping-related benefits
